## ❌ Failed Attempts

### Lezionario «today» alignment by text metrics
- **What:** scrolled so the date line sat at 10% of the screen, then at 0.6×line-height from the top.
- **Why it failed:** the grey title band sticks out above the letters by a different amount in the IT and RO PDFs; on his iPad IT was «2 mm too low», RO «7 mm».
  The IT PDF (lug-set 2026) also **misprints Thursday 24 as «Mer, 23 Set»**, so the date wasn't found at all.
- **Fix now:** `giornoDiOggi` counts day headings in order; `cimaDelTitolo` reads canvas pixels to find the real band top; re-aligns after 0.4/1.2/2.5 s;
  aligned to y=0. **The cause of the 7 mm Romanian offset on his iPad was never reproduced** — ask him to check again.

### Browser-pane screenshots / clicks while emulating a size
- Screenshots often stale (fix: `resize_window desktop` → set size again → wait 1.5 s → screenshot). Tool **clicks land at wrong coordinates** when a
  viewport size is emulated (they arrive off-screen) → test interactions with synthetic `PointerEvent`s via `javascript_tool` instead.

### Game answer grids squeezed in gio-fissa
- Old gio-fissa rules (`.dom-opz{flex:1 1 0}`, `.dom-opz .bt{flex:1 1 0;max-height…}`) squashed the 2-column answer grid of new games so the result box
  overlapped buttons C/D. Fixed with `body.gio-fissa .gn-pagina .dom-opz{flex:0 0 auto}` etc.

### Wheel labels
- Tangential labels were cut («DOMANDA», «RA ANCORA»); now short radial labels (`RUO_CORTI`, `corto`).
