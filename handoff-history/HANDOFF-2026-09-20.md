## ❌ Failed Attempts (this session — do not redo these)

### Keeping the old "proportional" cross-language anchor
- **What:** the v7.56 system (position of the mark as a fraction of the words of the day) was supposed to be
  enough if the buttons just copied notes.
- **Why it failed:** inside a long day it drifts by several words and at the edges lands on a different
  sentence — exactly what Ovidiu complained about. It had to be replaced by a real alignment of the two texts.

### Cognate matching by shared prefix alone
- **What:** two words count as the "same" if they share the first 4-5 letters.
- **Why it failed:** Italian «primogenitura» matched Romanian «primit» (nothing to do with each other) and
  dragged a whole sentence out of place. **Fix:** also require similar length (ratio ≥ 0.6 for a 4-letter
  prefix, ≥ 0.45 for 5+), see `_pesoAl`.

### Letting a single word match outweigh a sentence end
- **What:** concept/cognate matches weighed 3, sentence-final «.» weighed 1.2.
- **Why it failed:** «dall'opera svolta.» ↔ «lucrarea ta» (the *next* sentence's word) won over the full stop and
  shifted the boundary between two sentences. **Fix:** a real sentence end now weighs 3.2 (more than any single
  word); abbreviations («p.», «cap.») stay at 0.8.

### Emitting «.» and the closing quote as two separate signals
- **What:** one signal for the full stop, one for `”`.
- **Why it failed:** Italian writes `peccatore’.` and Romanian `păcătosul!».` — the two signals block each other
  in the chain (different order), and a wrong pairing won by 0.1 points. **Fix:** one single "after the word"
  signal carrying the strongest mark + a flag for the closing quote.

### Snapping the boundary to the nearest sentence start/end without limits
- **What:** after mapping, look ±4 words for a sentence boundary.
- **Why it failed:** it jumped **over** a certain anchor (e.g. back to the previous question's reference) when
  the Romanian line had no final period. **Fix:** snapping is now bounded by the two surrounding certain points,
  and a boundary that sits exactly on a certain point is only adjusted inside the extra text the other edition
  has there.

### Treating running footers as normal text
- **What:** nothing special for «Lecţii biblice pentru Şcoala de Sabat…».
- **Why it failed:** a highlight that crossed a page break swallowed the footer, and boundaries landed on it.
  **Fix:** a line repeated on ≥ 2 pages at the top/bottom, or a lone page number, is flagged `_AF_FUORI` and is
  never used as a signal nor highlighted.

### Trusting `paragrafiGiorno`'s paragraph starts
- **What:** using the `capo` flag as "start of a sentence".
- **Why it failed:** the footer absorbs the first paragraph of the next page, and a hyphenated word («consacra- /
  rea») gets flagged as a new paragraph. **Fix:** `_capoAl` ignores a paragraph start on a lowercase word;
  hyphenated words are rejoined before making signals.

### My own test sequence (not a program bug)
- **What:** a double-tap test that seemed to show slow taps opening the editor.
- **Why it failed:** the previous single-tap test had already armed `LET._dueTocchi`. Reset the state between
  gesture tests.

## ✅ Working Solutions

- **Align the two translations, don't estimate.** Signals that mean the same in both languages: verse/page
  numbers (7 points), weekday+date headers (8), question letters «a. b. c.» (3), real sentence ends (3.2),
  question marks (3.2), commas (0.5), quotes, paragraph starts, look-alike words (2–4) and a small dictionary
  (Dio/Dumnezeu, Salmi/Psalmi, Atti/Faptele, Giovanni/Ioan…). Two passes: strong signals with a wide band give a
  guide, then all signals in a narrow band around it. The best monotone chain is found with a Fenwick tree
  (~60 ms for a whole lesson).
- **A boundary may correspond to a *range*** in the other language (where it has «(cap. 18)» extra): store the
  first and last position; a mark that *starts* there starts after the extra text, one that *ends* there stops
  before it — and inside that extra text look for the real sentence start («Prin contemplare» for «Contemplando»).
- **When there is nothing to match, mark nothing** (`portaTratto` returns null) instead of marking at random.
- **Non-highlight notes**: pen and text notes hang from the nearest text line above (`_rigaSopra` → last word of
  that line) and are placed at the same distance under the corresponding line in the other language; a note put
  at the end of a line stays at the end of the line (`dx`); a pen stroke drawn *over* words follows the word.
- **Note size**: «Grandezza» now means "at a 900-pt-wide page" — the same unit the shared PDF already used — so
  `_pxTestoLez` makes notes scale with the page, and `grandezzaTestoLez` gives a new note the size of the text
  around the tap.
- **Testing without the Browser pane visible**: drive real gestures with synthetic `MouseEvent`/`PointerEvent` on
  `.let-note` / `.let-tx` and verify by reading the data (which words the rectangles cover), not by screenshot.
